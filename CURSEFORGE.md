# Jagodka – Community Addon for WoW Classic Era / Forever

Jagodka connects several allied guilds into **one community**. Members share chats, a member list, events, a calendar and a looking-for-group board, whichever guild they are in and even when they are not online together.

## Shared chat
- **General, Trade and LFG** chats shared across all community guilds, on their own secret channel
- **Officers chat** for the officers of all guilds, on a separate channel
- The community chat as a tab in your **game chat window**, which can be your only chat tab: choose in Settings what else shows there (whispers, guild, party, raid, system messages, game channels, and the community Trade, LFG and officers chats)
- Chat commands **/jnglobal, /jntrade, /jnlfg, /jnofficers** switch what you write to, or send one message from any chat tab
- Clickable item, spell and profession links
- Recent chat history is delivered when you log in
- Works alongside other chat addons

## Looking for group
- List yourself with your **roles**, up to **4 activities** (dungeons, raids, PvP, questing in any zone, or anything) and a short note
- Everyone in the community sees your listing within seconds; it lasts 3 hours and survives a relog
- Search, filter by activity and role, sort; whisper or invite with a right click
- A short note with an **[Open in LFG]** link appears on the community LFG chat when you list yourself
- **Invite players to your events** straight from the list: they get a whisper with an **[Open event]** link that opens the sign-up

## Events and calendar
- **Every member can organize** an event (officers more at once), with an instance, date, time and description
- Sign up with your **spec**, as Coming, Maybe or Not coming; mark yourself as raid leader
- A reminder 15 minutes before the start
- Monthly calendar view with filters and search
- Invite everyone who signed up to a group with one click
- Events and sign-ups stay in sync, even if you were offline when they changed

## Members and guilds
- Browse the members of every community guild, including those without the addon
- Search, sort and filter; right-click to whisper or invite
- Professions of members with the addon
- Your **character card**: level, guild, rank, professions, experience and an Armory link
- Side column: your events and LFG listing, officers online, guilds and classes at a glance

## For officers
- Mute players on the community chat (synced across all guilds)
- Monitoring tab with the state of the community, the connections between guilds and a recorded log for finding problems
- Remind players with an old addon version to update, and send big on-screen messages to the community

## Good to know
- Only guilds on the community list can use the addon
- At least one person with the addon must be online in a guild to connect it to the others
- Everyone should use the same addon version; the addon tells you when a newer one is out
- Built-in **Help** in English and Polish, with small "?" buttons next to every feature
